About

A tort lawsuit can be challenging, regardless of the merits of the allegations. Orf Legal Group (OLG) guides clients through the claims and litigation process with clear advice, careful case evaluation, and strong advocacy at every stage. Based in Olympia, Washington, OLG is committed to achieving the best possible outcome in every case it handles.

Integrity
The adversarial legal process can create difficult situations for attorneys and clients alike. OLG meets those challenges with open, honest communication and a vigorous defense in every case. We understand that litigation can be stressful, and we work to ease that burden by keeping clients informed, updated, and confident they are receiving the strongest possible representation.

Excellence
At OLG, every case is carefully evaluated to deliver the highest level of legal representation possible. We believe that attention to detail, thorough preparation, and strategic advocacy are essential to protecting each client’s interests and advancing their position as effectively as possible.
